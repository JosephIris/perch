Why Unified might win

The interleave gives you causality the tabs can't: "Found it — it's counting the wrong things" sits directly under six reads of perch.log. You see the conclusion and the evidence in one glance.

One scroll, one scrollbar, no mode-switching to answer "what's it up to". The ☰ button in the rail header hides the work rows — that's your Journal tab, as a density toggle rather than a tab.

Why Tabbed might win

Activity is 144 rows to Journal's 25. Interleaved, the prose gets outnumbered ~6:1 and the narrative you actually wanted starts to drown — exactly the "hard to scroll and follow" problem, just one level less noisy.

Changes is a state (what the tree looks like now), not an event. Cramming it into a chronological feed as a collapsed strip is a compromise; a tab lets it just be a list.

Behavior in both

Custom scrollbar — 10px, no track, thumb at 12% white → 24% on hover. Two new tokens.

Stick to latest — pinned to the bottom by default; scrolling up releases the pin and raises the jump pill. New content while unpinned doesn't yank you.

Keep all history — yes. Your biggest transcript ever (5.8 MB) extracts to just 296 beats + 342 tool calls. There's no budget worth spending here.

Dormant tabs — sleep, group, wake

Click Sleep on any active row (hover it), open the Idle chevron, and click a dormant row to wake it. Every state change is logged on the right. Nothing here is wired to the app — it's the flow, at real sizes and real motion.

ConceptWhat it is
Hollow ringAgentState.Idle — transient, per pane. A bare shell, or an agent that exited. Already exists.
DormantNew, persisted, per session. Set by the Sleep button, cleared on activation. Drives the Idle group — not the dot.
DormantAtStamp used to order the Idle group, newest at top.
WokeAtStamp used to seat a woken tab at the top of the active list.

Why a persisted flag and not just the dot: a woken bare shell reports Idle the moment it spawns. If the group were derived from the dot, that tab would fall straight back into Idle the instant you woke it. The flag is what you set; the dot is what the agent reports.
